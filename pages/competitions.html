Men's competition

Each season follows a structured format: a round-robin competition, followed by semi-finals weekend and a grand final weekend.

The RLBC Men's Competition is proudly the largest domestic rugby league competition in Canada, providing a high-quality and competitive environment for athletes across British Columbia. Clubs are based in the Lower Mainland and in Whistler.

Women's competition

As women's rugby league continues to grow in British Columbia, RLBC is committed to providing the best possible experience for female athletes.

Our women's competition is designed to balance skill development, competitive play and accessibility. The season features:

  • Multiple rugby 9s tournaments throughout June
  • A three-round 13-a-side competition in July

This format ensures that athletes receive ample field time while making it easier for new players to get involved and experience the game.

RLBC 9s

The RLBC 9s is our annual pre-season tournament, designed to give clubs a chance to hit the field, welcome new players and enjoy a fun day of rugby league competition.

The tournament includes both men's and women's divisions and has traditionally been run as a single-day event, making it an ideal opportunity for clubs to prepare for the upcoming season and for new players to be introduced to rugby league in a fast-paced, accessible format.

2026 tournament

The 2026 RLBC 9s took place on May 23 at Brockton Oval, bringing together clubs from across British Columbia for a full day of fast-paced rugby league action. Congratulations to the Vancouver Dragons, who took home the 2026 RLBC 9s trophy after an impressive tournament campaign.

Rugby League BC would like to thank all participating clubs, players, coaches, volunteers, referees and supporters who helped make the event a success.

Masters

Our Masters Competition is hitting the ground running. This new division gives experienced players an opportunity to stay active, enjoy competitive rugby league and be part of the growing rugby league community in British Columbia.

Masters games are for players aged 35 and over.
