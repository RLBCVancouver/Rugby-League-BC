Play

Whether you are new to the game or an experienced player, Rugby League British Columbia welcomes you to get involved and be part of our growing community.

RLBC, often in partnership with Canada Rugby League, hosts “Try Rugby League” events for those looking to experience the excitement and fast pace of rugby league in a fun and supportive environment.

Clubs are based in the Lower Mainland and the Sea-to-Sky corridor. If you want to know more about a specific club, we recommend contacting the club directly. Find a club near you.

If you are located outside the Lower Mainland, we are keen to connect and explore ways to support your involvement, whether through our annual Rugby League 9s Tournament or by assisting with the development of new clubs in other regions of the province.

For more information, contact Director of Rugby Stephen Lenahan at Stephen.Lenahan@rugbyleaguebc.ca.

Coach

Coaching is a great way to stay involved and give back to rugby league. RLBC coaches contribute at all levels of the sport, from community and grassroots programs to competitive and representative teams.

Rugby League British Columbia, in partnership with Canada Rugby League, is currently developing coaching certification courses and clear coaching criteria. These initiatives are designed to support coach development and ensure our coaches have the best possible opportunity to succeed on and off the field.

RLBC regularly hosts coaching seminars and development opportunities. For more information, contact Director of Rugby Stephen Lenahan at Stephen.Lenahan@rugbyleaguebc.ca.

Officiate

Officials are an integral part of the sport. Without them, games wouldn't be possible.

Rugby League British Columbia welcomes anyone interested in helping out as an official. If you'd like to get involved, contact Blake Stewart at blake.stewart@rugbyleaguebc.ca.

Officials courses

If you are interested in completing an officials course, contact the RLBC board for more information and guidance on available opportunities.

Volunteer

Passionate about rugby league? Interested in using your skills to help develop the sport, or looking to learn new skills while supporting the game? We'd love to hear from you.

Rugby League British Columbia is operated by a dedicated group of volunteers. Our work spans planning, development, event delivery, communication and fundraising, and support of all kinds, big or small, is always welcome. RLBC is actively working to promote representative team events, continue the development of the women's game, and expand opportunities in youth and wheelchair rugby league.

Every contribution helps grow the game in our province, even if your interests fall outside the opportunities listed below. Questions? Email board@rugbyleaguebc.ca.

Current opportunities

Membership Committee

Represents the interests of RLBC members, including players, coaches, officials and volunteers, ensuring their voices are heard within the organization.

Coaching Committee

Assists with organizing coaching clinics and seminars, and helps develop and implement coaching standards and criteria for community-level and representative coaches.

Representative Teams Committee

Supports the organization and operation of RLBC representative teams, including player selection processes, match planning and tours.

Women's Committee

Focuses on organizing women's rugby league events and supporting the continued growth of the women's game across British Columbia.

Youth Committee

Helps develop and deliver youth and grassroots programs, supporting long-term participation and growth of rugby league in the province.

Masters Committee

Supports the development of the masters competition and represents the interests of masters players within RLBC.

Partnerships Committee

Works to identify and secure sponsorship and partnership opportunities that help fund and grow rugby league in British Columbia.

Communications Committee

Supports content creation, communications and information sharing to keep members informed and engaged.

Social Committee

Helps organize member events and activities, from game-day beer gardens to end-of-season banquets and social gatherings.

Judiciary Panel

Reviews incidents and makes decisions related to player and coach eligibility and conduct following on-field matters.
