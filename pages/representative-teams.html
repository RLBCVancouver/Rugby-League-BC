Each year, these teams compete in an interprovincial series against Alberta, held over the September long weekend. The fixture provides a high-performance environment and an opportunity for athletes to test themselves at the next level.

RLBC representative teams also serve as a key pathway to Canada West selection and, ultimately, national team representation, supporting athlete development from provincial to international levels.

BC Silver Foxes

The BC Silver Foxes are our masters representative team. Masters rugby league is all about staying involved in the game and having fun.

Masters games are for those 35 and over, and always end in a 0–0 draw.

Interested in playing masters?

We are looking to put together a masters team to play a game or two. If you are interested in playing, email masters@rugbyleaguebc.ca.

More about masters rugby league

Masters rugby league is played in many places around the world. UK Masters Rugby League has great resources on the rules of the game, including a breakdown of the age-related shorts system.
