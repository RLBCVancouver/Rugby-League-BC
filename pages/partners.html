Founding partner opportunity

Sponsor rugby league in BC

Interested in helping grow rugby league in British Columbia while promoting your business? Rugby League British Columbia offers a variety of sponsorship packages for organizations and individuals looking to be part of our community.

We provide flexible sponsorship options that can be tailored to meet your needs. Thanks to our past and current sponsors, rugby league in BC continues to grow. We invite new partners to join us in making a lasting impact on communities and youth development.

To learn more about becoming a sponsor, email Richard Marshall at richard.marshall@rugbyleaguebc.ca.
