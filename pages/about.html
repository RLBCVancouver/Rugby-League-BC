Rugby league in BC

RLBC is a non-profit association and proudly administers the largest rugby league competition in Canada, supporting the growth of the sport from grassroots participation to representative pathways.

RLBC oversees and delivers multiple competitions and development programs across the province.

Our vision

“To govern, develop, and grow the sport of Rugby League throughout British Columbia.”

Our mission

“To sustainably develop Rugby League by delivering quality competitions and events, creating commercial value, growing participation and spectatorship, and advancing athlete development, while upholding the Laws, integrity, and long-term future of the game.”

Our history

Rugby league was reintroduced to British Columbia in 2011 with the formation of British Columbia Rugby League (BCRL).

In 2020, the organization undertook a strategic rebrand, officially becoming Rugby League British Columbia (RLBC) to better reflect its role as the provincial governing body for the sport.

During its first decade, the league featured clubs from across the province, including the Lower Mainland, Kelowna and Whistler, helping to establish a strong and competitive foundation for rugby league in British Columbia.

In 2021, RLBC reached a major milestone with the introduction of its first women's competition, marking an important step toward inclusivity and the continued growth of the game.

Governance

Rugby League British Columbia has made significant progress in recent years to establish a strong and sustainable foundation for future generations of rugby league in British Columbia.

Through the development of a strategic plan, a Long-Term Athlete Development (LTAD) model, coaching certification pathways and officiating education programs, RLBC is committed to building a well-governed and professionally supported sport.

These initiatives are designed to strengthen the game at every level and ensure that athletes, coaches, officials and volunteers are provided with the structure, education and support needed to succeed, both now and in the future.

In February 2020, the RLBC board met to develop a strategic plan aligned with its vision. The plan outlines clear goals and objectives within a three-year framework, providing direction, accountability and a sustainable pathway for the continued development of the sport.
